Help & transparency

Frequently asked questions

How FrameShift works, what AI renovation videos show, and how to use our design resources responsibly.

Are your videos real building projects?

No. FrameShift produces AI-generated design concepts and simulated renovation stories, clearly labelled as such. They are not recordings of actual construction work.

Are the manuals construction instructions?

The free checklist and guides support planning, design discussion and selecting questions for qualified professionals. They are not approved engineering designs, wiring diagrams, plumbing permits or site-specific installation instructions.

What rules apply to renovation?

Requirements depend on the country, local authority, building type, existing structure and scope. Permits, inspections and licensed trades may be required, including for electrical, plumbing, structural and glazing work.

Can I get a free resource?

Yes. Open the free Space Makeover Starter Checklist on this website and print or save it as a PDF.

Where can I buy products?

Our FrameShift Gumroad storefront is the product hub. Additional manuals are in development and will appear there after QA and launch testing.

Can I request a project idea?

Email frameshift74@gmail.com with the type of space and a short description. Never send account credentials or sensitive personal data.
